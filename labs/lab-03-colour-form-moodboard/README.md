# Lab 03 — Colour and Form Mood Board

**Course:** Canva Design Masterclass (`C156`)
**Version:** v1.0 · 30 September 2026

## Scenario

Develop a natural, premium direction for the Two Trees olive-oil range.

## Objective

Build a controlled mood board that communicates a brand personality through colour, shape and image treatment.

## Supplied image files

- `olive-leaf-outline.png`
- `olive-oil-varieties.jpg`
- `olives-on-branch.jpg`
- `rosemary-outline.png`

## Detailed procedure

1. Create a 1920×1080 mood-board page in Canva.
2. Write three brand attributes and one audience statement.
3. Sample or choose a five-colour palette with one dominant, two supporting, one accent and one neutral.
4. Add geometric and organic shapes that reinforce the selected attributes.
5. Place the supplied images without stretching and apply one consistent crop or colour treatment.
6. Add a concise annotation explaining each colour and form decision.
7. Review balance, contrast and negative space; remove any decorative element without a role.
8. Export the mood board as JPEG.

## Evidence to retain

One annotated mood-board JPEG and the five-colour palette.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every colour has a defined purpose.
- [ ] Images preserve aspect ratio and share a consistent treatment.
- [ ] Shape language matches the stated brand attributes.
- [ ] The board is coherent at thumbnail size.

## Reflection prompts

- Which decision most improved the message or focal hierarchy?
- Which visible design decision most improved the audience's understanding?
- What source, permission or provenance evidence must accompany the finished image?
