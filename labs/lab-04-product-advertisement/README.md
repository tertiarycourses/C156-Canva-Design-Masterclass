# Lab 04 — Product Advertisement Design

**Course:** Canva Design Masterclass (`C156`)
**Version:** v1.0 · 30 September 2026

## Scenario

Create a premium advertisement for one Two Trees olive-oil variety.

## Objective

Integrate product imagery, forms, typography and colour into a persuasive advertisement.

## Supplied image files

- `olive-oil-bold.png`
- `olive-oil-dressing.png`
- `two-trees-banner.png`
- `varieties-bold.png`

## Detailed procedure

1. Choose one product and write the audience, benefit and call-to-action.
2. Create a square social advertisement and establish margins and a grid.
3. Place the product as the focal element without stretching or obscuring the label.
4. Apply the Lab 3 palette with sufficient foreground/background contrast.
5. Use shapes to group supporting information and guide the eye toward the call-to-action.
6. Create one controlled variation using a different colour proportion or image crop.
7. Compare both versions against hierarchy, brand fit and readability.
8. Export the selected design as JPEG and retain the comparison image.

## Evidence to retain

Two design variants, comparison note and selected advertisement JPEG.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The product remains the primary focal point.
- [ ] Colour and form reflect the selected brand direction.
- [ ] The call-to-action is visible and readable.
- [ ] The exported JPEG contains no unintended crop or distortion.

## Reflection prompts

- Which decision most improved the message or focal hierarchy?
- Which visible design decision most improved the audience's understanding?
- What source, permission or provenance evidence must accompany the finished image?
