# Lab 07 — Aesthetic and Functional Audit

**Course:** Canva Design Masterclass (`C156`)
**Version:** v1.0 · 30 September 2026

## Scenario

Review the Two Trees product advertisement as if preparing it for stakeholder approval.

## Objective

Evaluate a design with a shared rubric and propose specific enhancements.

## Supplied image files

- `audit-banner.png`
- `audit-product.png`
- `audit-variety.png`

## Detailed procedure

1. Duplicate the Lab 4 design and label it Baseline.
2. Score hierarchy, balance, contrast, alignment, proximity, consistency and accessibility from 1 to 5.
3. For each score below 4, identify the visible feature and audience consequence.
4. Prioritise the top three issues by communication impact.
5. Write one specific revision for each priority issue.
6. Implement the highest-impact revision on a duplicated page.
7. Compare baseline and revised design at identical zoom and size.
8. Export a side-by-side review board as JPEG.

## Evidence to retain

Completed rubric and side-by-side baseline/revised JPEG.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every recommendation names a visible feature and expected effect.
- [ ] The highest-impact issue is addressed first.
- [ ] Comparison conditions are consistent.
- [ ] The revised version improves at least one rubric score with evidence.

## Reflection prompts

- Which decision most improved the message or focal hierarchy?
- Which visible design decision most improved the audience's understanding?
- What source, permission or provenance evidence must accompany the finished image?
