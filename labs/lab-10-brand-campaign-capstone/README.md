# Lab 10 — Collaborative Brand Campaign Capstone

**Course:** Canva Design Masterclass (`C156`)
**Version:** v1.0 · 30 September 2026

## Scenario

Produce a launch mini-campaign for the Two Trees olive-oil range and prepare it for team handoff.

## Objective

Integrate brief, mood board, advertisement, storyboard, critique and enhancement strategy into one coherent campaign.

## Supplied image files

- `olive-oil-basil.png`
- `olive-oil-delicate.jpg`
- `olive-oil-garlic.jpg`
- `olive-oil-rosemary.png`
- `olives-on-branch-2.jpg`
- `two-trees-logo-wide.png`

## Detailed procedure

1. Write the campaign brief with audience, message, formats, constraints and acceptance criteria.
2. Create a mood board and concise brand-rule page.
3. Produce one hero advertisement and one supporting social graphic.
4. Create a six-frame storyboard for a short launch sequence.
5. Organise pages with meaningful names and define owner/reviewer roles.
6. Conduct a structured peer critique using hierarchy, colour, typography and brand consistency criteria.
7. Record accepted feedback and revise at least one visual output.
8. Write an enhancement strategy naming changes, owners and expected effects.
9. Export the final campaign board and individual deliverables as high-quality JPEGs.
10. Verify every JPEG, then present the design rationale and revision evidence.

## Evidence to retain

Campaign brief, mood board, brand rules, two campaign visuals, storyboard, critique record, revised output, enhancement strategy and final JPEG package.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The campaign is visually coherent across all outputs.
- [ ] Evidence for every lab stage is present and traceable.
- [ ] At least one peer-led improvement is visible.
- [ ] All JPEGs reopen at the intended dimensions without distortion.

The structured peer review in Step 6 is the capstone reflection record.
